# Content and Rights

The [MIT licence](LICENSE) covers the **code** of this repository. It does not cover the texts, translations, images and scans the site publishes. Those keep the rights of their authors, translators and sources, as set out below.

## What You May Reuse

| Material | Status | Reuse |
|---|---|---|
| Works whose authors died more than 70 years ago | Public domain | Free for any use. Credit to kitabxana.az is welcome but not required |
| Translations whose translators died more than 70 years ago | Public domain | Free for any use |
| Works and translations by later authors and translators | Rights held by the author, the translator, their heirs or the publisher | Read them on the site. Do not republish them |
| Scans of the source editions | The edition's rights, as above | Offered for reference and checking the text |
| Author portraits and artwork | The licence shown with each image (often CC BY-SA or the Met's Open Access) | As that licence allows |
| Metadata the project made: author dates, work lists, tags, the literature tree | CC0 | Free for any use |

The site offers its own downloads (EPUB, TXT) only for public-domain works.

Every text records its source edition in `packages/parser/data` (`sourceId`, `sourceNote`). [packages/parser/data/README.md](packages/parser/data/README.md) lists every source and its terms.

## Rights Holders

If you hold the rights to something on the site and want it changed or taken down, write to **hello@azadbilik.org** or [open an issue](https://github.com/azadbilik/kitabxana/issues/new/choose). We answer every request.
