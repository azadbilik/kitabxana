bioEn: >-
  Aşıq Ümer (1621–1707) was a Crimean Tatar medieval poet of ashik and is one of
  the most famous representatives of the Turkic-speaking ashik poetry in
  general. Ashik poetry (Crimean Tatar: aşıq; lit. 'the person in love') is a
  special kind of literary oeuvre, the representatives of which – folk
  poets-singers – accompany their performances with playing the string-plucked
  musical instrument bağlama. Hence another name for this poetry – "bağlama
  poetry".


  He wrote mainly lyric poems (on mystical content and related to a soldier's
  theme) in the forms of Turkic folk poetry. Aşıq Ümer is also an author of
  poems that are traditional for classical oriental poetry – ghazals, rubaʿi
  etcetera. He greatly influenced the later poets-improvisers (ashiks).
