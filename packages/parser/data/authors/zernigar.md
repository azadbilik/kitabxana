bioEn: >-
  **Zarnigar** — a woman ashik from Derbent. According to legend, she bound
  forty-nine ashiks in a poetic contest; with her *qoshma* "Dərbəndə gəl" she
  challenges Ashik Valeh to the arena. Her *qoshma* remains in the second volume
  of Salman Mümtaz's collection «El şairləri» (Folk Poets), published in 1928.
