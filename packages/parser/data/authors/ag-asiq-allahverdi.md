bioEn: >-
  **Agh Ashiq Allahverdi** — Azerbaijani ashik and poet, teacher of Ashik Ali
  Gyzylvenkli.


  Before this, he practised the ashik art mainly in Sharur-Daralagez. In
  Daralagez the ashik art developed as well as in Goycha. According to the
  accounts of the elders of Goycha, Agh Ashik held lavish wedding ceremonies and
  competed with many famous ashiks in Nakhchivan, Sharur, Daralagez, Erivan,
  Goycha, Darachichek, Kalbajar, Lachin, Gazakh, Shamkir, Shamsaddin and
  Ganjabasar, and also in Baghdad, Basra, Tabriz, Bukhara and Urmia. He
  contested with Ashik Durakhan in Erivan and with Keshishoglu in Nakhchivan and
  Kichik Mazra, and won over all of them. In addition, he was the teacher of the
  well-known Ashik Ali Gyzylvenkli.
