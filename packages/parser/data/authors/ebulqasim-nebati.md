  Seyid Abulgasim Nabati (Persian: سید ابوالقاسم نباتی, Azerbaijani: Əbülqasım
  Nəbati, 1812–1873) was a 19th-century Iranian Azerbaijani poet.


  Very little information about his life is known, that is not even accurate as
  to the family he belonged to. What is known for certain is that Nabati
  graduated from high school, spent his youth in Arasbaran, later visited the
  tomb of Sheikh Shahabaddin in Ahar, after which he returned to his native
  Ushtibin, where he lived until his death. After his death, his poems, not all
  of which were published during his lifetime, or even recorded, were performed
  by locals in songs.
