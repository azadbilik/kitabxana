Each moment, the clever actor behind this curtain shows a strange scene on the curtain.

The minstrels are seated on the carpet, but there is none to dance. The sea is full of pearls, but there is no diver.

Silver, wealth, a crown, or a sword, are not grudged to thee, if thou dost not grudge them to thyself.

Even though thou wert equal to the angel Gabriel, fate is not niggardly; neither is divine power miserly.

However much thou takest, there is still more to take. His profits are greater than thine expenditure.

Travel on this road and note thy progress. Knock on this door and understand the mysteries.

Its stones are rubies, and its herbs are the philosopher’s stone. If thou dost not realise this, the fault is thine.

Here the controlling hand and the pen are broken, because such great treasure is behind this curtain.

Each moment a fruit comes from this garden; each is more beautiful than the preceding one.

In this treasury are many heart strings; each necklace is more beautiful than the other.

Each group of the travellers who walk one behind the other, is wiser than the preceding one.

Reason honours only reality; reason sets no store on age and youth.

It is said that, when a stone grows old, it becomes a ruby. Opinions differ on this point.

These people grow worse with age. In them there is nothing but noise, like an echo in the mountains.

Thy milk is an unwholesome poison to him who has seen thee suckling in infancy.

Justice is wanting in old age; the old do not wish well to the young.

A fresh rose gives great happiness. It is the old thorn which wounds.

Collyrium is made from unripe grape. A serpent becomes a dragon in old age.

An old brain cannot receive reason which inhabits the skull.

He who compiles the tables of the stars, does not make his predictions from an old almanack.

The old dogs who eat like lions, tear out the bowels of the gazelles wolfishly.

If I fear the old wolves, look upon my beauty and blame me not.

The old wound lightly, their wound is pleasant. What can I do with the nature of youth, which is fire?

Although youth is all wisdom, does it not also possess a strain of madness?

Some grey-haired men who lay claim to youth, claim to be black, when they are white.

I who scatter treasure like a rose, claim old age in youth.

The old are possessed by self-love. Enmity to self is friendship with God.

Call that new moon which thou sawest as a crescent, a full moon, when it is perfect.

When the palm tree grows tall, stretch thy hand far that it may reach the dates.

When the grain which is thrown into a corner, grows to an ear of corn, call it a grain no longer.

See that, when a pond becomes a lake from the water of the stream, thou dost not look upon it with the same eyes.

The sun looked with a different eye upon the night which entirely closed its eyes to the dawn.

A deadly enemy who is wise, is better than a foolish friend.

Do not look upon the sugar cane merely as vegetation; see how sweet it is.

Give thy heart to merit not to a boastful man; surrender to merit wherever it may be.

Though the oyster shell has much water, a pearl is produced by one drop of rain.

Much trouble of heart and soul is needed to procure a royal pearl.

Thy protection should sound its bugle for every banner which providence raises anew.

They have not yet ruined this village; nor have they yet rolled up this carpet.

Do not censure creation; beware, lest thou shouldst suffer the scourge like Satan.

Providence compels him to rebel who did not submit to its commands.
