Arise and roll up the carpet of the heaven, because there is no fidelity on this chequered backgammon board.

Do not seek the picture of thy heart’s desire in its union. Do not expect fair play, when it doubles its throw.

Why put one’s foot in this sea? Why lay one’s load on these waves?

The falcon said to the duck: “The country is beautiful.” It answered, saying: “Thank you, I am quite comfortable here.”

Oh thou, who art in this ship of sorrow, thy merchandise is the cause of thy destruction.

Throw thy burden overboard, because it cumbers thee; it will not give thee bread, unless it drowns thee.

There is no treasure of safety in this dustbin; there is no marrow of fidelity in this bone.

This world gives thee no grain of favour; dissolve thy partnership with it.

The provision in this tent is but a dirty bowl and an empty table.

Whoso looked at it, his mouth was sealed. Whoso spoke to it, his tongue was burnt.

There is naught in this litter but the sound of many bells. There is naught in this bowl, but there are many flies.

He who dipped his finger into this bowl, made of his skull the ring on his finger.

It is not right that in this village there should be perpetually distracted thought and troubled sleep.

Retreat into the house of non-existence; leave this ruined village.

Set the window of this house on fire. What gain is there in selling thy house to this old woman?

Why hast thou embraced this world? Thou hast not brought it forth from thy womb.

Draw thy pen across this world and live in happiness. Remove thyself and live peacefully far from it.

Thy road is long and thy halting place far away; prepare baggage and provision for thy journey,

Especially in this desert, full of demons, a hell which kills the heated and devours the thirsty.

The blood of the heart is the spring of its water of life; the sun is its salt.

Its salt is the wine of the saltless; the evil eyes yearn for it.

There is no water, and through this salt which looks like water, the courage of the heart is destroyed, and the heart of courage bleeds.

What will be the portion of the caravan of the senses on a road, at whose sight the heart bleeds?

In the heart of this desert, haunted by demons, the house of the heart is small, and the grief of the heart is great.

He who was content in this wilderness of the world’s desires, was saddened like the heart and burst like the gall bladder.

What use is there in this clay from hell? Arise and exchange hell for paradise.

To reduce this earthly form to dust, fate will tread thee down continually.

In the end, as is its wont, it will pass thee from hand to hand, until thou art lost.

Since we must return to the dust, why pass over this earth?

Do not trample anyone under thy foot, because fate has trampled many such as thee under its feet.

None has lived in this world for ever; none has possessed this decree of eternal life.

Do not tread on this thorn; arise, protect thyself from this thorn.

That impermanent abode of thine is a place of fear; why remain in a place of fear?

This world is transitory, do not deem it eternal; it is an autumn wind, do not think it the breeze of spring.
