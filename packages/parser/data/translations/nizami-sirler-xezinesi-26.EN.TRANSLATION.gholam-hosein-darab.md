Oh thou, who hast thrown down the shield of courage, thy refuge in alienation is thy demon.

Thou art proud of an empire which has no permanence. Thou livest but with a transitory life.

Thou art unprotected from the wine of the drunkards; thou art the plaything of the games of the stars.

Thou hast cast away the book and the sword and hast exchanged them for the cup and the flagon.

Thou hast taken in thy hand the mirror and the comb to admire thy tresses like a beautiful woman.

See what Rābe’eh did to the dog of the seven men with her own tresses.

Oh thou, by whom merit is shamed, be shamed by the virtue of a widow.

How long wilt thou lay claim to courage? Boast less, boast less, because thou art less than a woman.

Reason cannot dispense with virtue. No virtue is greater than justice.

This water was refreshed but not in thy stream. The mole was beautiful, but not on thy cheek.

Thou art not heaven, approve of good decrees. Fear high heaven greatly.

One should display only pure gems; one can make a good profit on this capital.

It brings misfortune to do injustice, to dishonour oneself, and to shed the blood of others.

Much has been claimed before now to bring together some great spirits.

Do justice and fear the spirit of men. Fear the arrow of the complaints of the oppressed.

Do not despise the spirit when it turns to God, because it has great results.

See what the unclean spirits of those two men did to Mahmud’s body;

Then see what the spirit of so many pure souls will do with thee on the day of judgment.

The travellers who follow the angels, are not slower than the tortoise on the road of inspiration.

Remove the sword of injustice from their path, that thou mayest not suffer their complaints by night.

Justice is the condition of sovereignty. Behold the condition of the world which is injustice.

He who did justice in this house for one night, adorned his house for the morrow.
