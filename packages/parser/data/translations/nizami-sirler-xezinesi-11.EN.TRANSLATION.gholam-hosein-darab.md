Oh thou, who art the glory of the essence of mankind, the sight of the eye of the world,

The heaven which gives thee continual victory, is pregnant in its nine wombs with one secret of thine.

The ears of the two fish above and beneath thee are the shell for the pearl of thy sword.

The moon which draws its sword by night, has thrown down its shield before thy sword.

The brightness of thy sword, which is like the waters of the Euphrates, has broken the jar of the water of life.

He who sleeps during thy deluge, would be carried away by the flood, even if he were like Noah.

Thy bowl gives understanding like that of Kei-khosrou and Jamshid. Thy face burns the moth of the sun.

Be lion-hearted; thou conquerest the brave. I said lion in error; thou conquerest lions.

Heaven fears thee more than it fears the lions of this thicket.

Who on the field of battle would have such a high heart and such daring as to boast to thee of valour and courage?

Thy hand of desire is absolute over all that exists under the blue sky.

Thou hast given office to many, but the angels alone gave thee office.

Fortune chose thee to be the end of time. The wind wrote Solomon on thy dust.

God who gave thee youth and empire, gave thee the empire; do with it what thou wilt.

Dust becomes gold by thy acceptance; poison becomes sugar by thy remembrance.

If Fereidun does not drink wine with thee, the snakes of Zahhāk will grow on his shoulders.

Drink wine; thou hast a minstrel and a cupbearer. Why grieve? Thou hast the eternal kingdom.

Thou art the protector of the empire and the refuge of kings; thou ownest the sword and the crown.

Though with the awe-inspiring sword thou tookest the crown and the throne,

Thou scatterest treasure like the Caliphs; thou givest crowns and takest away thrones.

The point of thy sword is over the crown. Why shouldst thou not take tribute from the kings?

That head on which thy foot rests, takes a throne. How fortunate is that heart in which thou hast a place.

The owl in thy era gives good fortune like the Phœnix. The head which reaches thy presence is lowered to the ground.

The notorious reprobate is guided. Because of thee, complaint has come to complain.

Under the hoof of thy steed, which is the pillar of the earth, thy enemy is nailed to the ground like a horseshoe.

The seven heavens are a casket for thy jewel; the eight paradises are a pennon on thy standard.

He who does not obey thy order, finds the crown on his head turn to a halter.

Thou art the master of all and every art. Thou art the soul of both worlds in one body.

Punish generosity; kindle the candle of speech.

Bestow the exalted robe of honour on servitude; grant the hope of acceptance to Nezāmi.

Though speech is nourishing and life-giving, it is lean when it reaches thy table.

This sea and this mine are without pearls and rubies; give them pearls from the hand and rubies from the lips,

And to him who is envious, give ungrudgingly rubies from the arrow and pearls from the sword.

Since heaven gave thee a happy omen, may the conclusion of thy work be acceptable.

On thy road are those who are prosperous and those who are destroyed. Prosperity is mine; destruction is the lot of thine enemy.

Thy victory has raised its head like a banner, and thy foe has lowered his head like a pen.
