Oh God! Thou wast from the beginning when we were not; Thou wilt live eternally and we shall perish.

The universe obeys Thine order; the shoulder of heaven carries Thy saddle cloth.

We are the homeless beggars at Thy door; we have the ring of Thy servitude in our ears.

We bear Thy brand, and kings do not accept the branded dog for the chase.

Thou wilt accept us, because we are from Thy garden. We are the dove which wears the collar of Thy servitude and the dog which bears Thy brand.

We do not expect aid from any helper; we have no other support but Thee.

All these hopes and fears are for Thy sake; therefore forgive us, oh generous One.

Help us, because we have no helper. If Thou dost drive us away, to whom shall we turn?

What is this tongue, and what is this speech? Speech and silence are both to be regretted.

What an immensity between my insignificance and the praise of God’s glory!

How dared this soul traverse this sea? How dared this heart drink from this spring?

We are helpless as those who are dumb, therefore we repeat: “He who knows God.”

Since I am ashamed of my useless speech, forgive me by Thy bounty.

Although we come before Thee in our destitution, yet we come hoping in Thee, oh God!

Befriend us, oh Helper of the sorrowful! Help us, oh Helper of the helpless!

The caravan has passed, see how far we lag behind. Oh Thou, our Helper, look on our helplessness!

With whom shall we take refuge? Thou art without equal. To whom shall we fly? Thou art our Helper.

We will not make a shrine except of Thy door; who will cherish us, if Thou wilt not?

Who begs as we do? Who laments more than we?

Forgive our sins, because we pray to Thee; help us, because we take refuge in Thee.

Oh God, Nezāmi’s name is honoured in Thee; his greatness comes from serving Thee.

Send the blessing of praise to his tongue; give his soul knowledge of Thee.
