Oh thou, who art more precious than the night of union, whose shadow is lighter than the dawn,

How long wilt thou sit in sorrow like a shadow? Arise, for a banner is better when it is raised.

When kings intend to start on a journey, they send their baggage on before them.

If thou art a king, resolve to start on the road; prepare for a better journey.

Send thy baggage on before thee; send on thy provision for to-morrow now.

Because of the prudence of the bee, its hive is full of honey.

The ants which manfully draw up in line, carry provisions for the morrow.

The heedless man, unless he is blind, is not below that bee and this ant.

He who wishes to enjoy worldly comfort, should lay in provision in summer for the winter.

Except thee and me, all who obey this instinct, are dealers in jewels of an hour.

No man’s spirit thinks of the future; no man’s vision sees more than the moment.

Our abode which is higher than the heavens, is the abode of foresight.

No matter how and where I search, no one has greater foresight than we.

Though the pleasures of the moment are sweeter than life, they are less sweet than the pleasures of foresight.

We who know the secrets of the heart, are jewellers, though we are made of clay.

We have a sign of what is to come, and we know the past.

We have read carefully from this table of clay the minute subtleties of the lesson of the nine spheres.

No one bears our brand; we were the first fruits of this garden.

On that day, when they sifted thy dust, they compounded it for the elixir of the heart.

With great pain and trouble thy dust is mixed; in the heart of this dust there are many treasures.

Recognise the due value of this dust; worship the dust, oh ungrateful one.

From thy coming and going to this place, learn the road to thine own abode.

What is the purpose of thy coming on this journey? What is the wisdom of returning thence?

In the beginning, when this empire did not bear thy name, and this ruined village was not thine abode,

Thou didst possess the glory of the angelic Phœnix; thou flewest in the zenith of heaven.

Though the power of the wing of thy love had no bounds, the eternal way, too, had no limit.

Thou wast weary and camest to this earth; thou threwest thy shadow onto this clay;

And again, when thou art tired of this prison, thou wilt tread on the skirt of the sun.

Though thou mayest leave all behind thee, thou wilt still go forward.

Thy work is only to progress; thou wilt not settle on one line.

In the time of generosity, thou art generous; thou art new and old in existence.

Leave this mother who kills her children; heed what the father has said to thee.

Look to thy father, oh simple minded man; hold to his traditions and see what he did.

One cannot expect ease and comfort, because it is not obtainable in this life.

If one moment of life were according to one’s heart’s desires, one’s wasted life could return again.

Grieve and pass on. Art thou made from other clay? What reason hast thou for happiness?

Be content, it is not to me or to thee that Providence said: “Be happy.”

We were created for toil and for profitless discourse.

On my life! We do not deem the give and take of the world to be real.

Why is thy face red like wine because of thy coming? Since all who come must depart.

How long will there be this compulsory arrival and departure?

It cannot be doubted that non-existence is nothingness; the doubt is in existence which is also nothingness.

Do not fly swiftly, since thou camest slowly; do not go quickly, because thou camest with difficulty.

A time will come when the trumpet will sound; they will stamp our die on a new coin.

They will refresh the drooping rose; they will bring together that which was scattered.

Oh thou, who art not ashamed of to-day, at least be ashamed of that other day.

For all these troubles which lie before us, for this patience of our wounded hearts,

Religion is our only remedy and the only steed for this desert.

See the difficulties of the road and do not lag behind; do not waver, oh enduring soul.

Hold the mirror of effort up before thee; look in it and protect thy face.

Repent, and God will forgive; do not enter submissive only to the divine power.
