In the Name of God, the most Merciful and Compassionate is the key to the door of the treasury of the wise.

The Name of God is the beginning of thought and the end of speech. Complete thy thought and speech in His Name.

Existent before all creation, more eternal than all eternities,

Ancient Lord of the eternal universe, Decorator of the neck of the Pen with a necklace,

Revealer of the secrets of the mysterious heaven, secret Goal of those who know the divine mysteries,

Source of every spring of liberality, Author of all existence,

He adorns the mountain with rubies, giving verdure to the earth and a ripple to water.

Guide to those who cultivate their souls, Source of light to those who partake of His daily bread,

He strings the fine thread of intellect with pearls and is the sight of the blind eye of reason.

He marks the brow of the saint. He crowns the kings of this world.

Destroyer of well matured plans, He it is who accepts excuses for shortcoming.

Protector of those who cry aloud in fear, Source of the counsel of the spiritually minded,

Beginning and end of Existence and Attributes, Creator and Destroyer of creation,

Compared with His Majesty which is greater than both worlds, our time from its beginning to its end is but an instant.

Who but God in this eternal universe could say: “Whose is the Empire?”

He was, when all that is high and low was not; He will be, when all that exists has come to an end.

Those who were nourished by eternal knowledge, have not solved this difficult problem.

Oh, what an ocean is His eternal knowledge! Oh, what a desert is His eternal kingdom!

His eternity has no beginning; neither has it any end.

The celestial maidens in the garden of thy form come from Him; the light of the sight of thine eyes comes from Him.

The struggle of every living being is to do service to His Godhead.

His Existence alone has permanence; He is sanctified who is not transient.

Gratitude is due to Him from the sun and the mountains for His thousand bounties to them.

When His munificence was veiled by light, the thorn was far from the rose and the cane from sweetness.

When by His liberality He created generosity, existence was freed from the bondage of non-existence.

For the sake of these few ruined villages, the work of heaven was entangled.

Until He had untied this knot which transcends the power of thought, the tresses of the night were not freed from the grasp of the day.

When He scattered the pearls of the necklace of heaven, He combed the dust of night from the tresses of non-existence.

He made seven knots on the girdle of the earth, when He made the spheres of heaven.

He made a robe for the sun and a cloak for the moon from this white cloth of day and this black cloth of night.

He loosened drops of rain from the heart of the sea; He drew the spring of life from the lips of heaven.

He poured the wine of dawn into the dark clay; He poured a draught of that wine into the mouth of the stone.

He made the lustre of the pearl and the richness of the ruby from the fire and water which He mingled together.

He placed the blood of the heart of the earth, shed on account of the severity of the wind, in the heart of the red ruby.

He made the garden of generosity as beautiful as the sky; He made the fame of the nightingale of speech reach to heaven.

He gave sweet dates to the palm tree of speech; He gave the oyster shell of the ear to the pearl of speech.

He veiled sleep. He clothed the water with the garment of life.

He spread the tresses of the earth on the shoulders of the world. He painted the mole of sin upon the face of Adam.

He cleansed dishonour from the face of gold. He washed the blood from the rose with the spring cloud.

He lightened the darkness of the skies by the stars. He entrusted the soul of the morning breeze to sweet herbs.

He placed the blood of the world in the heart of the clay. He placed the pulse of wisdom in the beat of the heart.

He set laughter on the lips to destroy grief. He seated Venus at the banquet of night’s minstrelsy.

The perfume of the night is diffused by His musk-sellers. The new moon wears the ring of His servitude.

A stone thrown from His royal tent broke the head of soaring eloquence.

Halting imagination travelled far and wide and returned from His door empty-handed.

The mind travelled far but did not find Him; the eye searched far and wide but found none like Him.

Reason entered, saying: “I have sought Him.” This was presumption; I chastised it.

Those drawn by the point of His compass join us in our search of Him.

The birds on the Tree of Paradise soar towards Him; those who walk in heaven knock also at the same door.

As for heaven, it wears the collar of His servitude, and as for the heart of the earth, it is full of love of Him.

Unity lives by His omnipotent name; eternity is the pillar of the throne of His empire.

The sighs of the weary are His favoured guests; His swift messengers are the feet of captives.

The heart which is purer than the soul, lays claim to being the dust of His threshold.

Compared with a rose of the garden, grown from the seed on the dust of His threshold, Eram is but an idle tale.

The dust of Nezāmi which exists by His aid, is soil for the seed of His unity.
