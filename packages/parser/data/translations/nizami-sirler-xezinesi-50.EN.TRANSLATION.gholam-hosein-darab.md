Oh thou, whose banner is raised by a breeze, and who lowerest it to that which is mere dust,

There is no village, and thou claimest to be a villager. There is no empire, and thou claimest the throne of Solomon.

Thou art not a sword. What are all these wounds? Thou art not a bell. What are these tinklings?

Scatter silver like the edge of the sword. Rise, be empty like the inside of a drum.

The devil draws thee to himself; thou hast not yet fallen. Do not give up the fight; thou art not dead but living.

Do not surrender to a Magian; do not claim courage, when thy sword is never drawn.

The eloquent are worthy to read the proclamation of empire. The Messiah is the recipient of the sneeze of Adam.

He who, like a moth, possesses true love, will strike single-handed at the army of fire.

Dive for a short time and die. Throw down the beggar’s robe and receive an empire.

By the living God, thy generosity which is a cloak for plunder, is not godly.

Be a lion; do not fear the kitchen cat. Be talc; do not fear the fires of hell.

If thou art depraved, hell fire is thy lawful portion. If thou art gold or a ruby, do not fear fire.

How much more pride, oh rogue of the dustbin? How much more self-glorification, oh thou bag of bones?

There have been others before us, who did not rest from seeking high position.

See what profit was theirs from that glory. There was a profit, but since it was lost, what profit was it?

If thou, a grain of dust, shouldst tread upon heaven in power and dignity like the sun and the moon,

Even if thou shouldst surpass that circle, since thou art dust, thou wilt fall.

So that thy head may not be cut off, as hair is cut, beware, do not set foot on this pinnacle.

Thou art not a bird, thou canst not fly. Unless thou strivest mightily, thou wilt not reach it.

Attack heaven valorously, so that astonishingly it may fall at thy feet.

Thou hast drunk wine, why blame the poison? Thou hast sinned, why blame fate?

Do not rail at fate, oh good man; fate has done no ill to thee and to me.

Fate made great efforts and performed great wonders to improve us.

Since we are of no account in the world, why should we blame fate?

Unless the ruby is lustrous, the king will not put the seal of acceptance upon it.

Stones are plentiful in the world; those which turn to rubies are rare.

The thorn and the jasmine are both vegetable growths. One is the thorn which damages sight; the other is collyrium.

Colour and scent do not leave the true rose, even if it has no help from the water of the stream.

Even if water could increase its benefits, how could it turn thorns and thistles into jasmine?

If the scheme were not thus established, the laws of the world would have been otherwise.

The affair rests in the hands of God and is not affected by our designs. He gives empire and riches to whom he wills in this world.

A man falls through lack of grace; those who are favoured, need have no fear in this world.

The fortune of him who seeks the divine empire, is ever high. Be the servant of that empire, wherever it is to be found.

This kingdom is bestowed by divine grace and not by worldly authority. They do not give an empire in jest.

Hold to those who are spiritually rich, so that thou mayest not need to search vainly.

Do not associate with the base; be accepted and honoured by the world.

Prosper with the good fortune of Orion. Break the walnut and test thy fortune.

Humble thyself, if thou seekest the empire. Free thyself from the tangled affairs of this world.

It is the pure water which is welcome. Aloe-wood is burnt, because it is knotted.

Follow the Heart; give thy heart to none. Thine own body is load enough on thy way.

How long wilt thou lay thy hand on another bough, saying: “Oh, that I had greater riches?”

Even if thou couldst grasp the whole world, since thou must leave it, why seek it?

Renounce greed, because it is the robber on the road of devotion. Contentment will kill thy greed.

The centre of this turquoise-coloured dome is open to thee and closed to imagination.

Either do not fear, grasp it; or vanquish it with one thought.

There is no knowledge left in the clay of humanity, no spirituality left in the whole world.

In the two picture books of these nine scribes, there is no figure which has reality.

Do not seek the friendship of the enemy of reality; do not seek the water of life in the mouth of a dragon.

A wise enemy who is the grief of one’s soul, is better than a foolish friend.
