A fruitseller who lived in Yaman, had a small fox to guard his goods.

It kept good watch on the road and guarded the warehouse of the greengrocer.

A thief played many tricks, but none of his arts availed here.

To hurry matters he closed his eyes. He slept and by sleeping induced the fox to sleep.

When the fox saw that the wolf was asleep, sleep overcame it, and it laid down its head.

The thief saw in that sleep his opportunity to take the purse; he came and plundered it.

He who sleeps on this road, loses either his head or his hat.

Arise Nezāmi; it is not the time to sleep; it is the time to say farewell to all.
