O thou, who wearest a veil from Medina and one from Mecca, how much longer shall the sun be behind a cloud?

If thou art a moon, show us a little of thy light; and if thou art a rose, send us perfume from thy garden.

Those who expect thee are dying of expectation. We are crying out against thee, oh help us.

Come to Irān, do not stay in Arabia; thou hast the light and dark steeds of night and day.

Adorn the Empire and refresh the world. Fill both worlds with thy voice.

Strike the die thyself, that the amirs may not strike it; read the proclamation thyself, that the proclaimers may be silenced.

The dust of thy feet gave a perfume to the faith; the wind of discord arose and blew away that perfume.

Withdraw authority from those who live at ease. Cleanse this pulpit from the impure.

They live in the house of demons, drive them out; throw them into the granary of non-existence.

Give them less provender, because they are gluttons; make the provender thine own, because they are plunderers.

We are all body, come thou and be our soul; we are all ants, be thou our Solomon.

On one side they make a breach in the faith, and on the other they lie in ambush.

Thou art the leader, why is the caravan alone? Thou holdest the centre, why is the standard elsewhere?

Either send an Ali into the ranks of the battle; or send an Omar on to the road of Satan.

Throw darkness upon the moon of Yaman. Raise thy head like a moon from the silk brocade.

Be the helper of the captives; destroy the hypocrites.

It is enough to sleep for five hundred and seventy years; the sun is high, hasten to the assembly.

Arise and order the Angel of Death to blow out these few candles.

Be the secret behind the curtain of mystery. We are all asleep, be thou awake.

Stretch out thy hand and help us all against the calamities of this transitory house.

Nothing that is thy pleasure is aught but good; none can hold thee responsible for thine actions.

If thou lookest favourably upon us, thou wilt suffice for all our needs.

Describe a circle with thy finger, so that whatever exists may be bestowed upon thee.

Who can intercede with thee on the day of judgment for forgiveness for a handful of dust?

If thou drawest back the veil, both worlds will come to ecstasy.

The brain of Nezāmi which seeks knowledge of thee, lives by the ambergris of thy perfume.

By his prayers, bestow a perfume of fidelity upon him. Give to a beggar an empire like that of Fereidun.
