Oh God! From Thee all existence has sprung; through Thee the impotent dust is strong.

Creation rests under Thy banner; we live through Thee, as Thou livest by Thine own Essence.

Thy Being has neither form nor body; Thou resemblest none, and none resembles Thee.

Thou art unchangeable, and Thou didst not and wilt not die.

We are all transient; eternity belongs to Thee alone. The high and sacred empire is Thine.

The earth is fixed by Thy command. Thou makest the azure vault without pillars.

Who but Thee gave the curve of the polo-stick to heaven? Who but Thee gave to the cauldron of the body the salt of life?

When Thy eternity calls on the chequer-board of night and day, who but Thee can say: “I am the Lord?”

Love could no longer endure the attraction of Thy name, if peace came not from Thee.

When Thy generosity took the road of the world, the earth bore a heavy load.

The world would have been ruptured, if it had not been born from the loins of Thy munificence.

The necklace of devotion is threaded by Thee; worship save of Thee is unlawful.

May he be silent who does not speak of Thee; may all be forgotten which is not in remembrance of Thee.

The cup-bearer of the night begs for Thy cup; the bird of dawn prays to Thy name.

Draw back the curtain and come out alone; even if I were that veil, rend it.

Show heaven its impotence; loosen the knots of the earth.

Erase the signs of the universe; change the forms of matter.

Give back the speech of the tongue to the Pen; discharge the debt of the earth to non-existence.

Delay the progress of tyrants; remove selfish desires from the seekers of jewels.

Break asunder this universe of six dimensions; pull down the nine-tiered pulpit of heaven.

Throw down the casket of the moon upon the terrestrial globe; throw the stone of Saturn at the cup of Venus.

Scatter this necklace which illuminates the night; break the wings of the bird of night and day.

Scrape this layer of clay off the earth, and say to this brick of the earth: “Cease to be.”

Wipe the dust of night from the brow of the universe; say to Jupiter: “Fall,” and to Leo: “Do not rise.”

How long wilt Thou allow this new scheme of the universe to continue? Bring back a veil of the old order.

Destroy the scheme and free the neck of heaven from the chain of motion and immobility.

Pour water upon the fire of injustice; lower pride to the dust.

Burn the tables of the astronomers; close the eyes of the Sun-worshippers.

Deprive this constellation of the orb of the moon; draw back this curtain from these speculations,

So that they may testify to Thy divinity and confess their own non-existence.

The birds gird their loins, saying: “We are Thy slaves”; roses are filled with life, saying: “We live by Thee.”

There is no blood money for him whom Thou killest, and there is no fine for whomsoever Thou hangest.

Thou makest the stage of the journey by night to be long; Thou bringest back the sun which has gone down.

Although Thou mayest be angry with many, none can complain.

Thou hast given the light of reason to the soul; Thou hast imparted the savour of the heart to the tongue.

From Thee heaven received its motion, the fixed star its immobility. The garden of existence received the water of life from Thee.

The airs and graces of the wild rose come not from the morning breeze, but from the dust of Thy feet, its collyrium.

The calyx of the lily, Thy cup, has tongue only for Thy praise.

Nezāmi, the slave, who sings the song of Thy unity, is in both worlds the dust of Thy road.

Make his mind prosperous by the knowledge of Thee; free his neck from the snare of sorrow.
