One midnight when the sun set the world-illuminating torch in motion,

The eyes of the nine heavens were his retinue; Venus and the moon carried his torch.

He left the seven spheres, the four quarters, and the six dimensions in the secret chamber of creation.

Day bade farewell to his footsteps; night was in ecstasy at his advent.

The eyes of the strangers were heavy with sleep, when he turned quickly away from sleep.

With the cage of the body, the bird of his heart flew away from this prison to its own resting-place.

The birds, namely, the angels, remained behind helpless. Heaven threw down its cloak.

His body was lighter than the heart; his divine bird flew with the cage.

As he moved on step by step, he was seized and carried further and further towards divine bliss.

Since both worlds had their eyes on him, they bowed their heads adoring him.

His feet carried him hundreds of stages as one stage, because he had the highest honour.

His exalted steed threw down its saddle-cloth upon the shoulder of all existence.

The sea of the earth became a mine and he its pearl. The heaven bore him as its crown.

Taurus took the jewels of that dark perfumed night from the Bull of the earth.

He took the crown from Cancer and the belt from Orion as gifts for that journey.

The Virgo which received fresh hyacinth from him, threw the cluster at Leo.

Venus, who weighs the night, held Libra in her hand to estimate the honour of his night.

Libra bowed down to the glory of that night, because she had not weights enough in her scales to weigh its dignity.

His healing breath poured balm on the tail of this Scorpion of the azure realm.

When he hit the target with the arrow from the bow, the poison left the kid which was on his table.

He became Joseph of the bucket like the sun; he became Jonah of the fish like the water bucket.

When he set the throne of the Pleiades on the sign Aries, the army of flowers pitched its tents on the plain.

The pleasure house of the earth was decked with the colours of spring by the rose of that exalted garden of Paradise.

He recited the ten verses of courtesy from the Koran; he asked pardon of the prophets for his approach.

His footsteps rent the curtain of the stars. The shoulders of the angels bore his banner.

Midnight was filled with the musk of his lips. The crescent moon was the shoe cast by his steed.

The canter of Borāq was like lightning in the dark night of that event.

Like a partridge, the falcon flew dove-like with the glory of the Phœnix.

The Tree of Paradise touched the front of his robe. High heaven laid hands on his skirt.

The night turned into day; lo, what a wonderful day! The rose turned into cypress; lo, what a wonderful spring!

Amongst all the roses and narcissi of that garden, his narcissus had the collyrium which takes away false vision.

He was passed on like a rose from hand to hand up this turquoise-carpeted stair, till he reached the foot of the Throne.

His fellow travellers threw down their shields; they broke their pinions and shed their feathers.

He in amazement, like the strangers on the road, knocked at the door of that Threshold.

The veiled angels, His doorkeepers, with one accord helped him down from his litter.

He went on that road which must be travelled alone; one foot knew not where the other would tread.

All but him remained on the Threshold of that Mystery; even he lost consciousness of himself.

His foot was the crown of existence; the heavens had need of that provender.

When he drew his pen through all words, he raised his banner above the heavens.

The lord of life urged the body along the road, so long as his physical body drew the breath of life.

When the journey to heaven ended, the heart and the soul were charged with their own work.

The body hastened to the place of its origin. Vision became such that imagination could not follow it.

The eyes which are fit to look upon the Eternal Light will not lower themselves to vain thoughts.

The road of Eternity was before his feet. The curtain of physical creation was withdrawn.

When he travelled the endless road, he raised his head above the veil of nature.

His high spirits reached a stage beyond time and space through the great enlightenment of his heart.

Jealousy laid hold on him from behind that curtain; at the same time bewilderment held him back.

The hand of Union drew back the curtain from the glorious door of the house of Beauty.

His feet transferred their function to his head. His soul gazed on that Vision.

He travelled without the labour of his feet; he sprang, but was not allowed a resting place.

As words leave the lips, so he departed from himself; therefore his greeting was accepted.

He saw the sign of that Light which knows no decline, with eyes which were beyond imagination.

The vision of Him is free from accident and matter, because He is beyond accident and matter.

He saw God as the Absolute, and this is acceptable; God can be seen.

One must not deny that he saw with his eyes; whoso does not admit his vision is blind.

The Prophet saw Him, not with a different eye, but with this physical eye.

The vision of that curtain was not spacial; the journey on that road was timeless.

Whoso was permitted to see behind that curtain, went on the way which is not a road.

He is, but not in any place; He who is bound by space is not God.

Do not deny His permanence; it is blasphemy. Do not limit Him to space; it is ignorance.

He drank the wine which God had prepared; he poured a draught of that wine into our clay.

Eternal Grace was the companion of his soul. Divine Compassion was the cherisher; he was the cherished.

His lips were adorned with a sweet smile; he asked favours for his people.

His spirit was enriched by that Treasure; all his aims were realised.

He was strengthened by that Audience; he faced the toils of this world.

He returned from that journey which demands love. He went and came back in an instant of time.

Oh thou, whose word is a seal on our tongue! Thy scent is the elixir of our life.

Complete the cycle of generosity; entrust the completion of speech to Nezāmi.
