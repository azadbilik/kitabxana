Some counterfeiters met together and made a new counterfeit die.

Know their outward appearance to be tin like the inside. Guard thy words from them.

To thy face they are more agreeable than the daylight; behind thy back they are more deceitful than darkness,

Smoother than a candle, knottier than aloewood; smooth in outward appearance, but cunning in reality.

They are unjust and uncharitable; they find fault, and they complain.

They have learnt of love merely from the lips of others; they have stored up revenge heap upon heap.

They are eager, but more withered than a heart; they are alive, but as dead as their own hearts.

Do not test their friendship on the touchstone of thy heart. Thou art not drunk, do not put thy foot in this mire.

They echo like the mountain; do not tell them thy secrets. If thou desirest secrecy, do not give them voice.

They boast that they may gain thine affection; they strive to gain from thee.

Since that friendship is due to their need, may the curse of God be on such amity.

Any speech which has a selfish object, is friendship which incites to enmity.

Friendship which is based on division, is not friendship but enmity.

What will a friend call thy poison? A sweetmeat. What will a friend call thy faults? Virtues.

A friend is a healing salve. If he is not, cease to associate with the unworthy.

It is a cat which eats its own kittens from love of its own kind.

Who is a friend? He who keeps secrets. All these divulge secrets like time.

They all plan how they can surpass thee, and by what schemes they may lessen the value of thy coin.

Outwardly their reins are tied to thee; in the time of necessity they necessarily depart.

If the appearance of a friendship is denied by thy heart, it is enmity.

How can the body know who is thy friend? The heart knows who is faithful.

Thou hast one heart and a thousand heartaches, one withered rose and a thousand wounds from thorns.

There are a thousand kingdoms but only one Fereidun. There is much ambergris, but only few enjoy it.

Everything in this world divulges secrets. Only thy heart is the confidant of thy secrets.

If thy heart does not lock up that secret, how canst thou expect it to be kept in the hearts of others?

If thy heart is not transparent, and this is wrong, why is thy secret like a sun in the desert?

If thy heart does not tell thy secret by its transparency, why does a glass show the wine it holds?

Since friendship is a necessity, do not withhold thy secret from thy friend.

Since thou hast set foot on this empire, strive to gain a partner.

Unless thou knowest the soul of thy friend, do not waste the jewel of thine own secrets.
