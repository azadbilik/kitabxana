---
title: Privacy
description: What Kitabxana.az keeps about readers, and how to delete it.
---
You don't need an account to read Kitabxana.az. An account is only for putting books on your shelf, keeping lists, marking what you've read and picking up where you stopped.

## What we keep

- **Your email address**, to sign you in. If you sign in with Google, we also receive your name and profile picture from Google.
- **Your shelf**: the works, books and authors you save, your lists, your read marks and where you stopped in each work.
- **Your sign-in session**: one cookie, set in your browser only after you sign in, and its record on the server (when it was used and the browser's name). It lasts 90 days.

We keep no passwords: you sign in with Google or with a one-time code sent to your email. The code is valid for 10 minutes.

## What we don't do

No advertising, tracking or analytics cookies. We don't sell or pass your data to anyone. Sign-in codes are sent through [Resend](https://resend.com); when you sign in with Google, Google confirms it's you.

## Deleting your account

On your shelf, **Settings → Delete my account for good** removes your account and everything tied to it (shelf, lists, reading positions, sessions) at once and for good.

Questions are welcome through the [contact page](/en/contact).
