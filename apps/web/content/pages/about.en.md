Kitabxana.az is an open online library of Azerbaijani literary heritage. Here you can read the works of poets, writers and thinkers in the original and in translation.

## What's here

- **Works.** Every poem and every chapter has its own page. You can read a divan or a long poem straight through, from beginning to end.
- **Original and translation side by side.** Texts in Azerbaijani, Persian and Arabic, together with their translations.
- **Authors.** Their lives, their era and a list of their works.

## Why

Nizami, Khaqani, Mahsati, Nasimi and Fuzuli wrote not only in Azerbaijani but also in Persian and Arabic. Many of these texts survive only in print editions or are scattered across different websites. Kitabxana.az brings them together in one place and keeps them free for everyone.

## Sources

Texts come from public-domain editions and open libraries: Wikisource, Ganjoor and print editions. Each work shows its source. If you spot a mistake, [let us know](/en/contact).

## Account and privacy

An account is optional: every text can be read without signing in. If you sign in, we keep only your email address (and, with Google, your name and picture), your shelf, lists, read marks and the place you stopped in each text — nothing else. None of it is shown or passed to anyone. You can delete the account, with all of it, at any time in [Settings](/en/refim/ayarlar).

## How to help

Send us texts, translations or information about authors: [Contribute](/en/contribute). Editors review every submission.

Kitabxana.az is a non-commercial project.
