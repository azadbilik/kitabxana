Kitabxana.az is meant to be read by everyone. The measure we hold the site to is WCAG 2.1, level AA. The settings above change the whole site for you; this page says what else is there, and what is not there yet.

## What works

- **Keyboard.** Everything can be reached and used without a mouse. The first Tab on any page shows a link that skips to the text. On a reading page ← and → open the previous and next work, and Escape closes any panel.
- **Screen readers.** Pages have headings and landmarks, buttons and fields have names, and text in another language or script is marked as such, so Persian is read as Persian and right to left.
- **Text size.** The site follows the text size set in your browser and stays usable at 200% zoom and on a 320-pixel-wide screen. The **Aa** button on a reading page sets the size, line spacing, column width and typeface of the text being read.
- **Colour.** Light and dark themes; text contrast of at least 4.5:1 in both, 7:1 with high contrast on. Nothing is said by colour alone.
- **Motion.** There is very little, and none if your system asks for reduced motion or you switch it off above.
- **The dictionary.** A tap on a word of an Azerbaijani text opens its dictionary entry. From the keyboard the same dictionary is in the **Aa** panel: type the word under “Look up a word”.
- **Without an account.** Reading needs no sign-in, and signing in needs no password to remember: a code by email, or Google.

## What does not work yet

- **Scanned books.** The PDF of a source book offered for download is a photograph of its pages; many have no text a screen reader can read. The text of the same work on the site is the accessible version.
- **Texts typed from scans.** Some were read by machine and proofread by people; a misread letter may remain, and a screen reader will say it as written.
- **Spaced-out words.** Some old editions set emphasis by s p a c i n g the letters, and the text keeps it; a screen reader spells such a word out letter by letter.
- **Pictures in the galleries.** Titles of museum pieces are given in English only, and the pictures have no fuller description than their title.
- **Old-script texts.** A work in the Arabic alphabet is not always accompanied by a version in the modern Latin one.

## How this was checked

Each kind of page is checked by an automated test (axe-core) in both themes, on a desktop and a phone screen, every time the site is built, and by hand with a keyboard alone. For screen readers we read each kind of page as the browser hands it to one (names, headings, landmarks, languages, what is announced); it has not yet been listened to through with VoiceOver or NVDA. Last full review: 18 November 2026.
